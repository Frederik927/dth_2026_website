# Which opensource project would be useful in your major, and why?


## Voyant Tools 


According to its website, “Voyant Tools is a web-based text reading and analysis environment”. The platform was created in 2003 by Stéfan Sinclair and Geoffrey Rockwell. It is designed for scholars and the general public to facilitate a more effective workflow when analysing great amounts of data. 
To understand cultural differences and how they are rooted, there is often a need of reading between the lines. Hence, Voyant Tools can help unveiling patterns and highlining themes in online texts, making it a valuable tool for conducting different intercultural analyses. It could for example assist in making a thematic analysis, by generating word clouds of the most used terms, which subsequently could be coded and analysed. Furthermore, it enables you to make a visual representation of the text by, for an example, creating graphs and grids, displaying word patterns and themes in the text.

### My verdict

As Voyant Tool is a well-known and respected open source project, that has, among others, won the Zampolli Prize for its achievement in the digital humanities, I would rely on it for my thesis. Furthermore, it is also recognised by Aarhus University as a valid tool for data analysis, further strengthening its credibility. Though it would optimise the workflow and help keeping an overview of the process, I would be able to leave it and still manage to conduct my analysis.
